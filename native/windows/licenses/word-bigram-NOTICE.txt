Zhizi word bigram table (word-bigram-v1)

A pruned, Kneser-Ney smoothed word bigram table used only to build whole-sentence
candidates for inputs that mix full pinyin with bare initials. It contains word
pairs and log probabilities computed from the following corpora; it contains no
running text from them.

- Chinese Wikipedia (sampled ~40 million characters). License: CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . This table, as a derived
  statistical work, is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- ASAP (Chinese review corpus), Apache License 2.0.
- Tatoeba (Chinese sentences), CC BY 2.0 FR, https://tatoeba.org .

Word segmentation used the Zhizi lexicon (see LICENSE-LEXICON).
Built by scripts/lexicon/build_word_bigram.py.
